Breakout Model

Who is about to get better? Every NBA player 25 or younger with 500+ minutes in 2025-26, projected for 2026-27 with an 80% range. Ratings are LEBRON, a public all-in-one rating from BBall Index in points per 100 possessions: 0 is an average player, about -2 is replacement level, +2 or more is a clear starter. A Ghost Screen Analytics model, not an NBA stat. The tests were written before the model was run.

How to read it

2025-26
The player's LEBRON last season.
Projected
Expected 2026-27 LEBRON. The dark tick on each range.
Change
Projected minus 2025-26. Green is a projected jump, red a projected drop.
80% range
The span expected to hold for about four of every five players. It is wide on purpose. Young players swing a lot from year to year. The hollow circle is the 2025-26 rating.

Who is left out. The model only scores players who stay in the league and play 300+ minutes the next season. Players who vanish are not in the test or the projections.

    What it is built on

    Sample: player-seasons tested, 2016-17 to 2025-26, from LEBRON ratings of players 25 or younger with 500+ minutes, paired with the next season. Each test season used only earlier seasons for training. Inputs: current, offensive, defensive and last-year LEBRON, age, minutes, years since the draft, draft slot and position group. Regular season only.

    The rules, code, data and results are in the breakout-model folder on GitHub. Passing bars on ten seasons is not proof the model will keep working.
