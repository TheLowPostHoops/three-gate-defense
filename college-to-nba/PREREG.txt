GHOST SCREEN COLLEGE-TO-NBA MODEL: rules written before any model was run (2026-10-14)

What it does
- Takes a prospect's college numbers and draft slot and predicts how good he will be in NBA seasons 2 to 4, with an 80% range.
- Scores the 2026 rookie class and the 2027 draft board.

Outcome (what "good" means)
- NBA rating = LEBRON (BBall Index), minutes-weighted over the three seasons ending in the draft year + 2, +3 and +4.
- Players with fewer than 1,500 minutes in that window are padded with replacement-level minutes (rating -2.0) up to 1,500.
  A player who never reached the NBA therefore scores -2.0. Nobody is dropped for failing.
- Only drafted players (picks 1 to 60) with at least one Division I college season are in the sample. International and
  non-college picks are left out and the count is reported.

Features (fixed now)
- Draft slot (log of pick number).
- Final college season: age at draft, height, class year, share of team minutes, usage, true shooting, offensive rating,
  rebound, assist, turnover, steal and block rates, three-point attempts per shot, free-throw rate, 2P%, 3P%, FT%.
- Model: ridge regression with the penalty chosen by cross-validation inside the training years only.
- Baseline to beat: the same ridge using draft slot only.

Testing (walk-forward, no peeking)
- Test classes 2016 to 2022. A class is predicted using only classes whose three-season window had ended before that draft
  (class D uses classes up to D - 4).
- 80% range = prediction plus or minus 1.28 times the spread of training-year errors.

Pre-set bars (each pass or fail, all reported)
C1 Accuracy. Mean absolute error of the full model is at least 5% lower than the draft-slot-only baseline on pooled held-out players.
C2 Consistency. The full model beats the baseline in at least 4 of the 7 test classes.
C3 Ranking. Spearman correlation of prediction with outcome beats the baseline by at least 0.03.
C4 Late-pick finds. Among picks 21 to 60, the top quarter by full-model prediction has a higher average outcome than the top
   quarter by baseline prediction.
C5 Range honesty. 80% ranges cover between 75% and 85% of held-out players.
C6 Not luck. Shuffling college numbers among players in the same class 200 times, the real accuracy gain must be better than
   95% of the shuffles.
C7 College stats carry signal alone. The model without draft slot has a mean absolute error at least 10% below "predict the average".

Rules
- No feature, penalty, or sample change after seeing test results. A bar that fails is reported as failed.
- Passing is not proof of future accuracy: 7 classes, a few hundred players.
- Data: Barttorvik college statistics, LEBRON ratings, draft results. Playoffs not used.
