Three Gates: rim protection

A shot at the rim has to get through three gates: Redirect (the shots teams settle for away from the rim), Deter (how rarely they even try at the rim) and Contest (how often rim shots miss). Every defender graded on each, with ranges, not ranks.

How every model here works
  1. A pass/fail bar is written down before each test is run, so the result cannot be bent afterward.
  2. Only what clears its bar goes into a ranking or a projection. The rest is shown as a description and labeled.
  3. Every number comes with a range, and every result says what it passed or failed.
