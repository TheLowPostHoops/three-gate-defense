College to NBA

Every 2026 draft pick and the 2027 draft board, scored from college numbers and draft slot, each with an 80% range. A Ghost Screen Analytics model, not an NBA stat. The tests were written before the model was run.

    The projections

    How to read it

    Projected NBA rating
    The expected level in NBA seasons 2 to 4, measured with LEBRON (a public all-in-one rating from BBall Index, in points per 100 possessions). Zero is an average NBA player, about -2 is replacement level (a player who barely plays or never makes the league counts as -2), and +2 or more is a clear starter.
    Slot only
    What the draft position alone would have predicted. The hollow circle on each range.
    Model
    Draft position plus the final college season: age, height, class year, minutes share, usage, shooting splits, rebounding, assists, turnovers, steals and blocks. The dark tick on each range.
    80% range
    The span the model expects to hold for about four of every five players. It is wide on purpose. Nobody in a class of 60 is a sure thing.

    2027 caveat. The 2027 board uses the public Tankathon big board rank as the draft slot and last season's college numbers (the 2026-27 season has not been played). Freshmen, players coming from outside college basketball and anyone without a 2025-26 college season are scored from slot alone and tagged slot only. Treat that class as a rough first look.

    What it is built on

    Sample: players drafted from 2008 to 2022 with a Division I season (international and non-college picks are left out), their final college season from Barttorvik, and their NBA rating over seasons 2 to 4. The test predicted each class from 2016 to 2022 using only earlier classes whose three NBA seasons had finished, so nothing in a test was seen in training.

    The rules, the code, the data and every result, including the first run, are in the college-to-nba folder on GitHub. A passed bar is not proof the model will keep working: it is seven classes and a few hundred players.
