BREAKOUT MODEL: RULES WRITTEN BEFORE THE MODEL WAS RUN
Written 2026-10-15. Nothing below was changed after the first run.

QUESTION
For a young NBA player, how much better or worse will he be next season than he is now? Does anything beyond his current rating help predict that?

DATA
LEBRON ratings from BBall Index, seasons 2009-10 to 2025-26 (file lebron_master.csv; "year" is the season's ending year). Draft position from the draft lists already in the college-to-nba folder.

SAMPLE
Every player-season (season t) with at least 500 minutes and an age of 25 or younger, paired with the same player's rating in season t+1. A pair counts only if the player played at least 300 minutes in t+1. Players who left the league or barely played are not scored, and that limit is disclosed with the results.

WHAT THE MODEL PREDICTS
LEBRON in season t+1.

FEATURES
Current LEBRON, offensive and defensive LEBRON, age, log of minutes, last season's LEBRON when it exists (otherwise the current one), years since the draft, log of draft pick (undrafted = 61), and position group.

MODEL
Ridge regression with standardized features. The penalty is picked by leave-one-season-out testing inside the training years only.

BASELINES
1. Persistence: next season equals this season.
2. Mean-reversion baseline: the same ridge method using current LEBRON alone.

TEST
Walk-forward. For each test season t from 2016 to 2025 (target seasons 2017 to 2026), the model trains only on pairs whose target season is already finished (target year <= t). The 80% range is the prediction plus or minus 1.2816 times the standard deviation of out-of-fold training errors.

THE SEVEN BARS (pass or fail, decided in advance)
B1 Accuracy: the model's mean absolute error is at least 3% below the mean-reversion baseline.
B2 Consistency: the model beats the mean-reversion baseline in at least 7 of the 10 test seasons.
B3 Beats persistence: the model's error is at least 10% below the persistence baseline.
B4 Finds jumps: among the 10% of each test season's players with the highest predicted gain (predicted minus current rating), the actual average gain is at least 0.20 higher than the same group chosen by the mean-reversion baseline.
B5 Range honesty: the 80% ranges contain between 75% and 85% of actual outcomes.
B6 Not luck: the real gain over the baseline is larger than 95% of 200 runs in which the extra features are shuffled among players in the same season.
B7 Breakout calls: players the model projects to gain 1.0 or more LEBRON points actually gain at least 0.5 on average, and at least 60% of them improve.

REPORTING
Every bar is reported pass or fail, including failures. If a data problem is found and fixed, both runs are published and the bars stay the same.

OUTPUT
Projections for 2026-27 for every player 25 or younger with at least 500 minutes in 2025-26, with ranges, and the list of biggest projected jumps.
